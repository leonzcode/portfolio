# Leon Clash: credits

Leon Clash is an original game. Its name, cards, numbers, rules text and interface are its own. The pictures and sounds below come from openly licensed sources. Each source page was opened and its own licence field read; where a work offers several licences we use it under the one named here.

The character sprite sheet (`assets/atlas0.webp`) is built from the parts below, recoloured and combined by `tools/clash/build_sprites.py`. Because some of its parts are CC BY-SA 3.0, the combined sprite sheet is shared under **CC BY-SA 3.0** too (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

## Drawn for this game

The Snow Golem and Snowlings, the Penguins, the Groomer and the Snowplow, the buildings (Snow Cannon, Flare Tower, Lift Tower, Cornice, Snow Catapult, Igloo, Snow Fence), the spell pictures, the paraglider's canopy, the lodges, huts, bridges, stream and field are drawn by code in this repository (`tools/clash/build_sprites.py`, `public/clash/js/render.js`).
