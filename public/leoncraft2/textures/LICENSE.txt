Leoncraft 2 - block textures

The block textures in this folder (terrain_albedo.png, terrain_normal.png,
terrain_spec.png, manifest.json, the colour maps, the sun and the moon) are
made from four resource packs: the two below, and REFI Textures and
Bauniclonia (credited under "Item pictures"), which give the terrain, wood,
ore, glass and farm blocks listed in tools/leoncraft2/data/open_pack_map.json.
Each texture's colours are then moved towards the game's own average and
spread (six numbers per texture, colour statistics only, no pixels).

- "Pixel Perfection Community Edition", by XSSheep and its contributors
  (block, item and entity textures for blocks up to Minecraft 1.16):
  https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE
- "Pixel Perfection Legacy", by Nova_Wostra, continuing XSSheep's Pixel
  Perfection (block textures for blocks added since 1.17: deepslate, tuff,
  calcite, copper, cherry, mangrove, bamboo, pale oak, mud, sculk, resin,
  sulfur, cinnabar, poplar and the rest):
  https://modrinth.com/resourcepack/pixel-perfection-legacy
  https://github.com/NovaWostra/Pixel-Perfection-Chorus-Eddit

Both are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Only the block textures of Pixel Perfection Legacy are used.

Changes made for Leoncraft 2:
- the textures are packed into vertical strips of 16x16 layers (one layer per
  texture, or per animation frame); textures wider than 16 pixels are
  downscaled (nearest neighbour), and only a few keep their animation;
- a few blocks drawn from small atlases (sniffer egg) use a 16x16 piece of
  them;
- chests, beds, shulker boxes, signs and mob heads are drawn by the game as
  boxes, so their faces are cut out of Pixel Perfection CE's entity textures
  and composed into 16x16 layers (chest, trapped chest, ender chest, the 16
  beds and shulker boxes, eight sign woods, five mob heads); the player head
  is taken from the game's own original skin (below);
- copper chests are the wooden chest recoloured to each copper stage; signs
  for mangrove, cherry, pale oak, poplar and bamboo are the oak sign
  recoloured to their planks; pointed dripstone is Legacy's sulfur spike
  recoloured to dripstone; the dragon head is a stand-in made from obsidian;
- door icons the packs do not have are made from the door's own two halves;
- normal, ambient-occlusion, height, smoothness, reflectance, subsurface and
  emission maps are generated from each texture (terrain_normal.png and
  terrain_spec.png).

Checked against the game's own files. A pixel-by-pixel comparison with
Minecraft's textures (October 2026) found that a few files in the packs are
Mojang's pixels, copied or traced, although the packs carry an open licence.
None of those are used:
- the nether portal and the wither rose come from Pixel Perfection Legacy,
  whose versions are original, instead of Pixel Perfection CE;
- the tripwire, the end portal, the jigsaw and structure blocks and the grass
  colour map are our own drawings (tools/leoncraft2/art/);
- the conduit, bamboo, honey block, dried ghast, seven of the glazed
  terracottas and parts of the loom, grindstone, lectern, cherry log top, bee
  nest, beehive, bell, smithing table, chiseled bookshelf and TNT are
  replaced by the packs' own original textures, recoloured or reused.
After that, no layer of the atlas matches a Mojang texture, in whole or in
part. Like any texture pack, many textures still follow the layout of the
block they show (a log's rings, a lantern's frame).

These textures, including the changes above, are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.

The scripts that build them: tools/leoncraft2/build_textures.py and
tools/leoncraft2/build_blocks.py in the portfolio repository.


Player skin (skin/default.png)

The default player skin is original work drawn for Leoncraft 2 (pixel by
pixel, from scratch). It is not based on, traced from or recoloured from any
Mojang skin or any other existing skin. It may be used under the same
licence as the rest of this folder, CC BY-SA 4.0.


GUI art (gui/)

The menu and inventory art in gui/ (icons.png, options_background.png,
world_selection.png, checkbox.png,
container/inventory.png, container/creative_inventory/, and widgets.png
apart from the parts named below) is copied unchanged from
"Pixel Perfection Community Edition", by XSSheep and its contributors:
https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The pack's title art (its logo and edition wordmarks), presets and panorama
are not used.

gui/font_ascii.png is that pack's font sheet (font/ascii.png), recoloured from
its cream colour to white so the game can tint it; it stays CC BY-SA 4.0.

In widgets.png the hotbar, the hotbar selector, the buttons and the language
button, and all of accessibility.png, are our own grey drawings
(tools/leoncraft2/build_gui.py), not the pack's gold ones.

The LEONCRAFT title and its BROWSER EDITION plate are drawn by the game at load
(js/ui/menus.js) from our own letter shapes, the stone texture above and the
font sheet above, so they are adapted material under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.

The container screens in gui/container/ (crafting_table.png, generic_54.png,
furnace.png, smoker.png, blast_furnace.png, hopper.png, dispenser.png,
shulker_box.png, enchanting_table.png, anvil.png, grindstone.png,
smithing.png, stonecutter.png, brewing_stand.png) are copied unchanged from
Pixel Perfection Community Edition, CC BY-SA 4.0. A pixel comparison with the
game's own screens (every version from 1.16.5 to 26.3) found none of them to be
copies; they share the game's layout, as any pack's screens do.


Item pictures (items.png)

items.png holds one 16x16 picture for every item that is not a block (in the
order of ITEM_TEX in js/itemdata.js), the eleven sizes of the experience orb,
the separate layers of the pictures the game colours per item (a potion's
liquid, a tipped arrow's head, dyed leather and wolf armour), and the frames
of the compass, the recovery compass and the clock. Made by
tools/leoncraft2/build_items.py from six resource packs:

- "REFI Textures", by MysticTempest (drawn from scratch for Luanti games,
  Mineclonia among them): dyes, coal, raw ores, food, the compass, the mace,
  the trident and others:
  https://content.luanti.org/packages/MysticTempest/refi_textures/
  https://github.com/MysticTempest/REFI_Textures
- "Refined Textures", by awuuwa (a fork of REFI Textures): bread, cooked
  chicken and the glistering melon slice:
  https://content.luanti.org/packages/awuuwa/refined_textures/
- "Bauniclonia", by Mirtilo (the Baunilha pack made for Mineclonia): every
  tool and armour piece, the ingots, gems, meat, buckets and others:
  https://content.luanti.org/packages/Mirtilo/bauniclonia/
  https://codeberg.org/mirtilo/Bauniclonia
- "Pixel ImPerfection", by bramaudi (Pixel Perfection reworked for
  Mineclonia): the blaze rod, the totem of undying, the heart of the sea and
  magma cream:
  https://content.luanti.org/packages/bramaudi/pixel_imperfection/
  https://codeberg.org/mineclonia/pixel_imperfection
- Pixel Perfection Community Edition (the experience orb, from its entity
  textures; the shield, drawn from its shield texture; and items up to
  1.16), and
- Pixel Perfection Legacy (spawn eggs, boats, harnesses, bundles, books,
  music discs, smithing templates, pottery sherds, spears and the rest),

all six CC BY-SA 4.0 (links above). tools/leoncraft2/data/item_sources.json
names the pack and file of every picture. Changes: each picture's colours are
moved towards the game's own average and spread (six numbers per picture,
colour statistics only, no pixels), fully or partly; many pictures are moved
by up to two pixels, and some mirrored, so they sit in the slot where the
game's picture does (the outlines compared; nothing is redrawn); the
compass, the recovery compass and the clock keep every frame (the game turns
them to where they point), other animated pictures their first frame; wider
pictures are scaled down to 16x16;
leather horse armour, potions, tipped arrows, firework stars and filled maps
are their grey base tinted the default colour with their overlay on top (as
the game draws them), and dyed leather is Bauniclonia's grey leather times
the dye; the shield is Pixel Perfection CE's shield texture drawn as the
game draws the shield in the inventory (its model, turned and scaled the
same way); the turtle helmet is Bauniclonia's leather cap recoloured; the
enchanted golden apple and the debug stick use the golden apple and the
stick.

Checked against the game's own files. Every chosen picture was compared with
Minecraft's own item texture (26.1.2, October 2026): no more than 15% of its
pixels may equal the game's at any shift of up to two pixels or mirrored, and
its fine detail may not follow the game's (a correlation of 0.75 as drawn, or
0.9 at any shift), where the pack drew it and where it is placed; traces keep
the detail even when recoloured. These were found to be Mojang's pixels,
copied or traced, and are not used: from Pixel Perfection CE, dried kelp,
leather horse armour, the turtle scute, the pufferfish and the trident, and
the compass (from both packs). Nothing turtle is taken from Pixel Perfection
CE (its turtle armour texture is the game's own), and the FamiliarCraft pack
was not used at all (its tools are the game's, recoloured). Pixel
ImPerfection's book follows the game's detail, so no book is taken from that
pack. Where a full colour pull would raise a drawing's detail past the limit
(the iron, diamond and golden leggings, the tipped arrow), it is pulled
partly or not at all.

A wider check (October 2026) compared every picture with every item picture
of every game version from 1.8.9 to 26.1.2, whatever its name: identical
pixels, and the inside detail wherever the outline is all but the same. It
found more pack files that are the game's pictures, and none of them is
used: Pixel Perfection CE's phantom membrane and nautilus shell (the 1.13
pictures, recoloured) and the "Miner 16px" pack's ingots (the 1.8 ingot; its
apple has the 1.8 outline too), so nothing at all is taken from Miner 16px.
The other pictures it pointed at are shapes so simple that any drawing
shares their outline (a stick, the leggings); each was looked at and is drawn
differently. Like any texture pack, many pictures still follow the layout of
the object they show (a sword is a diagonal blade over a guard and grip).

These pictures, including the changes above, are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.


Armour textures (armor/)

armor/<material>_layer_1.png (helmet, chestplate and boots) and
_layer_2.png (leggings), 64x32 in the game's layout, for leather, chainmail,
iron, gold, diamond, netherite and copper, and turtle_layer_1.png (the turtle
shell), are made by tools/leoncraft2/build_items.py from Bauniclonia's armour
textures (by Mirtilo, CC BY-SA 4.0, link above). Changes: each piece's own
64x32 sheet is laid onto one sheet per layer, the helmet moved from the hat
box to the head box; the colours are moved towards the game's own average and
spread (colour statistics only); leather_layer_N.png is Bauniclonia's grey
leather, to be multiplied by the dye colour (or by undyed leather's brown),
and leather_layer_2_overlay.png the leggings' waist band in Bauniclonia's
undyed leather (not recoloured), drawn over the dyed leggings untinted; the
turtle shell is the leather cap recoloured green. A pixel comparison with the
game's own armour textures found none of these to be copies. Pixel Perfection CE's turtle
texture is identical to the game's, and Pixel Perfection Legacy's turtle and
REFI Textures' iron, gold and diamond armour follow the game's too closely, so
none of those are used.

These textures, including the changes above, are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0.
