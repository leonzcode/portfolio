Leoncraft 2 - block textures

The block textures in this folder (terrain_albedo.png, terrain_normal.png,
terrain_spec.png, the colour maps, the sun and the moon) are made from
"Pixel Perfection Community Edition", by XSSheep and its contributors:
https://github.com/Athemis/PixelPerfectionCE

Pixel Perfection CE is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes made for Leoncraft 2:
- the 16x16 textures are packed into vertical strips (one layer per texture);
- normal, ambient-occlusion, height, smoothness, reflectance, subsurface and
  emission maps are generated from each texture (terrain_normal.png and
  terrain_spec.png);
- textures for blocks the pack does not have (deepslate and its ores, tuff,
  calcite, copper ore, cherry and mangrove wood and leaves, moss, mud, packed
  mud, powder snow, smooth basalt) are recoloured from the pack's own.

These textures, including the changes above, are shared under the same
licence, CC BY-SA 4.0. They are not Mojang's textures.

The script that builds them: tools/leoncraft2/build_textures.py in the
portfolio repository.
