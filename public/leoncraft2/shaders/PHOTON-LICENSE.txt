Leoncraft 2 - code from Photon Shaders

Leoncraft 2's RIP Computer shader level ports three parts of Photon Shaders
by SixthSurge (https://github.com/sixthsurge/photon, downloaded October 2026)
to its own WebGL pipeline, changed to fit it:

- the wind on plants and leaves: gusts read from a noise texture drifting with
  the wind and a wobble of three harmonics at each vertex's own phase, stronger
  in rain (include/vertex/displacement.glsl, get_wind_displacement; here
  js/render/glsl.js photonWind)
- plants pushed away from the player (animate_vertex), here given to everyone
  walking near (js/render/glsl.js pushAway)
- puddles and wet ground in the rain: puddles where a noise is high on flat
  open ground, darker and mirror smooth, the rain's ripples from three taps of
  a scrolling noise (include/surface/rain_puddles.glsl; here
  js/render/materials.js PUDDLES, with their reflections in
  js/render/post.js puddleLook)
