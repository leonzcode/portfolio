# Leon Freeski credits

- Rider models (`models/*.glb`, region masks `*_m.png`): Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library, https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox, Microsoft Research and the Rocketbox team. MIT licence (text below). Recoloured into ski clothes in the game. The hair (always under the helmet or hat) and the far copy of the body are taken out of the files (2026-11-11), and `models/*_lo.glb` are copies for the Low graphics setting with the pictures made smaller (512 px, WebP); the meshes and skeleton are the same.
- three.js r180 (MIT), shared from `/car/vendor/three/`.
- Snowboard (`models/board/board.glb`, the board): "Intermediate Advanced Snowboard" by Final Render Animation Studio (cleowillo), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/intermediate-advanced-snowboard-267e04a025434d7d8587ec2ee60ad62e, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Scaled to 1.55 m; its normal and roughness maps left out.
- Snowboard boots (`models/board/board.glb`, the boots): from "Snowboard #3DST34" by James, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/snowboard-3dst34-cdb9ab4c0bc64515872d739a9f5d421a, CC BY 4.0. A photogrammetry scan; only the two boots are used, cut off its board, simplified and given a lit material.
- Snow scooter (`models/scoot/scoot.glb`): the deck, neck and bars from "street scooter" by JamesFowler, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/street-scooter-94b873b7773f4028b4eccd8ebd04d86f, CC BY 4.0; its wheels taken off and two shortened copies of the snowboard above put under it.
- Snowbike (`models/bike/bike.glb`): the frame, saddle, fork and bars from "BMX Bike" by erdmdgdln, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmx-bike-fe10f12853044affbdc9e835eabc2c81, CC BY 4.0; its wheels, chain and pedals taken off, simplified, and narrowed copies of the snowboard above put under it and under the rider's feet.
- Sled (`models/sled/sled.glb`): "Sled" by Poly by Google, https://poly.pizza/m/bydgit--BYd, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Scaled to 1.05 m.
- Park bench (`models/park/bench.glb`, the wooden benches on the Jump Line's takeoffs): "Low Poly Old Red Painted Wooden Bench" by Kuutti Siitonen (kuuttisiitonen), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-old-red-painted-wooden-bench-0c0fd2ba8ba64d7987f7607be22b8ec4, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its textures cut to 512 px WebP and its roughness map left out; in the game each bench is stretched to its length between its legs, made wider, and its legs reach down to the ramp.
- Skies (`sky/*.jpg`): Poly Haven pure skies, CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license), turned into small pictures for the game (the sun's disc painted out, the brightness evened out, only the sky above 6 degrees under the horizon kept; in November 2026 the patches left where seven of the suns were painted out were filled again smoothly from the sky round them, and the night photo's compression blocks round the moon smoothed). Qwantani Dawn, Sunrise, Morning, Mid Morning, Noon, Afternoon, Late Afternoon, Sunset, Dusk 1 and Dusk 2 (Pure Sky), photographed by Greg Zaal, processed by Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/qwantani_noon_puresky (and the others by name); Kloofendal 48d Partly Cloudy (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/kloofendal_48d_partly_cloudy_puresky; Belfast Sunset (Pure Sky), Dimitrios Savva, Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/belfast_sunset_puresky; Snow Field (Pure Sky), Sergej Majboroda and Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_field_puresky; Kloppenheim 02 (Pure Sky), Greg Zaal and Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_02_puresky (the night).
- Snow surface (`tex/snow_grain.jpg`, `tex/snow_lumps.jpg`, normal and height maps): ambientCG "Snow 005" (https://ambientcg.com/a/Snow005) and "Snow 007 A" (https://ambientcg.com/a/Snow007A), CC0 (https://docs.ambientcg.com/license/). Cut to 512 px, normal and height packed into one picture each.

- Rider's gear (`models/gear/`, chosen in the locker), all CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), each checked on its Sketchfab page:
  - Helmet (`helmet_c.glb`): "Bicycle Hat" by MR.Sirapop (Sirapop.Satemrn), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bicycle-hat-660183838e9f4a16b53043e639ad8609. Scaled to the head; its colours replaced by the rider's.
  - Goggles (`goggles.glb`, the frame and lens): "Ski Goggles" by BlurryRender (arjunmano10), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ski-goggles-29bf30fb9c4d43548106c095505054a2. Its strap is replaced by one made in the game.
  - Beanie (`beanie.glb`): "Low-poly beanie" by Iggy3D, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/low-poly-beanie-f1d328dc5d49429e8b381ca3c38a25c4. Texture cut to 512 px.
  - Skis "Twin" (`ski_a.glb`) and "Pow" (`ski_b.glb`): one ski each from "Park Ski Pair" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/park-ski-pair-25966e44862a4b9892c61fd8087e453f) and "Pow Ski Pair" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pow-ski-pair-28d8c8d0308d453985813a17b3995d14) by calebhammel. Scaled to 1.78 m and coloured in the game; the bindings on them are made in the game.
  - Poles (`pole.glb`): the grip and shaft of "HorkidUI Custom Ski Pole Concept" by KylePCT, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horkidui-custom-ski-pole-concept-7adec51aeab34cb39b89ccc687502e7b, its shaft lengthened and its top cut off; the basket and strap ring are made for this game.
  - Binding plate (`binding.glb`, under each boot): "Skibinding" by bgk, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/skibinding-8326b141af7b4448bcf68e4bf922f13f. Simplified from 36,360 to 582 triangles and given a plain material; the toe and heel pieces on it are made for this game.
- Chairlift (`models/lift/lift.glb`: the chairs, towers, stations and bull wheels): "Chair Lift" by Kagelok, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/afd305cdd4cb450f80b59f24b4b3e236, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Cut into its parts (its resort sign, panels, snow and cable left out), simplified, and its textures baked into one 1024 px WebP; the cable, the stations' snow pads and the chairs' running are made in the game.
- The Jump Line's T-bar operator huts (`models/tbar/hut.glb`): "Ticket Booth" by daedaljs, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7e12559eed534b5699c4262e7e1a6bb1, CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Its four parts merged into one mesh of its own vertex colours, the glass made opaque and its normal map left out. The rest of the T-bar (towers, sheave trains, rope, hangers with their spring boxes, cords and T-bars, the bull wheels and the stations) is made in the game: no openly licensed model of one was found.
- Scuffs and scratches on the park's tubes, boxes and steel rails (`models/park/plastic_rough.webp`, `models/park/steel_rough.webp`): from ambientCG's "Plastic 010" (its roughness map) and "Scratches 005" (its scratch mask, made into roughness), https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Plastic010 and https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Scratches005, CC0 1.0. Cut to 512 px WebP.
- Trees (`tex/fir_atlas.webp`): from Poly Haven's "Fir Tree 01" by Rob Tuytel (photography) and Rico Cilliers (modelling), https://polyhaven.com/a/fir_tree_01, CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). Four of its twig sprays (its twig colour and alpha pictures) and a strip of its bark cut into one 512 px picture, with a strip of its sprays heaped up dark for the middle of the crowns; the trees themselves (trunks, whorls of branch cards, snow on them) are made in the game.
- Freeride's rock (`tex/rock_col.webp`: colour with height in alpha; `tex/rock_nrm.webp`: normal with ambient occlusion in blue): Poly Haven's "Dark Rock 02" by Amal Kumar, https://polyhaven.com/a/dark_rock_02, CC0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). Cut to 512 px WebP and packed two maps to a picture; on the cliffs, and where rock shows through Freeride's snow.
- Everything else (the park's tubes, boxes and rails, the jib line, the terrain, the ski boots, the snowboard bindings, the trees' shapes, the snow's shader and tracks, the floodlight towers, the poses and tricks, Freeride's far ranges and the light its mountain casts and holds) is made for this game.
