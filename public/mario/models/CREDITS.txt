Models
------
Every .glb in this folder is from Kenney's Racing Kit (version 2.0), taken
unmodified from https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit

Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal), public domain.
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Free for personal, educational and commercial use. Crediting Kenney is not
required; it is here so anyone reading this knows what these files are and where
they came from.

The karts themselves are not from here - they are built in code in kart.js, one
per racer, so each one can carry its driver's colours.
