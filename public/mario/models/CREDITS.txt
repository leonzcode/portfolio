Models
------
Every .glb in this folder is from Kenney's Racing Kit, taken unmodified from
https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit

Forty of them: the barriers, stands, trees and tents that were here already,
plus the catch fencing, guard rails, billboards, floodlights, marshal flags,
pit buildings, radar dishes, pylons and grass tufts that fill the band behind
the barrier. Downloading them beat modelling them.

http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Free for personal, educational and commercial use. Crediting Kenney is not
required; it is here so anyone reading this knows what these files are and where
they came from.

The drivers are Kenney's Mini Characters, in chars/, also CC0 and also taken
unmodified, from https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters (their licence is
chars/License.txt). Eight of the twelve are used, one per racer; drivers.js
poses each one at the wheel and bakes the pose when the page loads.

Also CC0, also Kenney, also unmodified, each folder with its own licence:
- kit/      Car Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit): the race car, the future
            racer and the go-kart bodies, and the racing wheel. models.js reads
            their colours into the vertices so a racer's paint can replace them.
- nature/   Nature Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit): the trees, cacti,
            rocks, flowers and logs round the meadow, dunes and ember circuits.
- holiday/  Holiday Kit (https://kenney.nl/assets/holiday-kit): the snowy pines,
            snowmen, drifts and lanterns.
- city/     City Kit Commercial (https://kenney.nl/assets/city-kit-commercial):
            the buildings round Neon Mile.

The karts themselves are not from here. They are built in code in parts.js and
kart.js out of bodies, wheels and trails, so every part can be painted from the
racer driving it and every part can change how the car handles.
