Models
------
Every .glb in this folder is from Kenney's Racing Kit, taken unmodified from
https://kenney.nl/assets/racing-kit

Forty of them: the barriers, stands, trees and tents that were here already,
plus the catch fencing, guard rails, billboards, floodlights, marshal flags,
pit buildings, radar dishes, pylons and grass tufts that fill the band behind
the barrier. Downloading them beat modelling them.

Licence: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal), public domain.
http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Free for personal, educational and commercial use. Crediting Kenney is not
required; it is here so anyone reading this knows what these files are and where
they came from.

The karts themselves are not from here. They are built in code in parts.js and
kart.js out of bodies, wheels and trails, so every part can be painted from the
racer driving it and every part can change how the car handles.
